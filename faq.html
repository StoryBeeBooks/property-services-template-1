Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our services and policies.

General Inquiries

What areas do you serve? +

Monarch Property Care proudly serves the entire Greater Toronto Area (GTA), including Toronto, North York, Etobicoke, Scarborough, Mississauga, Oakville, Richmond Hill, Markham, and Vaughan.

Are you insured and WSIB compliant? +

Yes, absolutely. We carry $2M in comprehensive liability insurance and all our staff are fully covered under WSIB. We prioritize safety and professionalism in every job we undertake.

How do I get a quote? +

Getting a quote is simple! You can fill out the form on our Contact page, call us directly, or send an email. For standard services like window cleaning or gutter cleaning, we can often provide an estimate over the phone or via email using satellite imagery. For larger projects like deck restoration, we may schedule a quick, free site visit.

Do I need to be home during the service? +

For exterior services (window cleaning, pressure washing, eavestrough cleaning, snow removal), you do not need to be home as long as we have access to the water supply and the work areas (e.g., unlocked gates). For interior window cleaning, we require someone to be present to grant access.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Interac e-Transfer, and cash. Payment is typically due upon completion of the service to ensure your complete satisfaction.

What is your satisfaction guarantee? +

We stand behind our work 100%. If you are not completely satisfied with any aspect of our service, please let us know within 24 hours, and we will return to make it right at no extra cost.

Window Cleaning

Does your window cleaning service include screens and tracks? +

Yes! Our premium window cleaning service includes wiping down the frames and sills. Screen cleaning and deep track detailing can be added to your package for a complete restoration.

What happens if it rains on my scheduled service day? +

Rain water is actually quite pure and doesn't typically spot windows; it's the dirt on the glass that causes spots. However, for heavy storms or high winds, we will reschedule your appointment for the safety of our crew and to ensure the best possible results.

Can you remove hard water stains? +

Yes, we have specialized solutions and techniques to remove stubborn hard water stains and mineral deposits from your glass. Please mention this when booking so we can allocate the necessary time.

Eavestrough & Gutter Cleaning

Do you clean up the debris from gutters? +

Absolutely. We hand-remove debris from your eavestroughs and bag it for disposal. We never throw debris on your lawn or flower beds. We also flush the downspouts with water to ensure they are flowing freely.

How often should I have my gutters cleaned? +

We recommend cleaning your eavestroughs at least twice a year: once in the late spring (after seeds/blossoms fall) and once in late autumn (after the leaves have fallen) to prevent winter freezing and blockages.

Pressure Washing & Decks

Will pressure washing damage my siding or deck? +

Not with Monarch. We use a "soft wash" technique for delicate surfaces like vinyl siding and wood decks. This involves using specialized cleaning solutions to lift dirt and algae, followed by a low-pressure rinse, ensuring a deep clean without the risk of damage.

Are your cleaning chemicals safe for my plants and pets? +

Yes, we use eco-friendly, biodegradable cleaning solutions. We also take extra care to pre-soak and rinse surrounding vegetation to ensure your landscaping remains protected.

How long do I need to stay off my deck after staining? +

We recommend waiting 24 to 48 hours before walking on the deck or replacing furniture, depending on the humidity and temperature. We will provide specific instructions based on the product used.

Snow Removal

When do you come out to clear snow? +

Our crews are dispatched when snow accumulation reaches 5cm (2 inches). For continuous storms, we make multiple passes to ensure your driveway remains accessible.

Do you offer seasonal contracts? +

Yes, we offer flat-rate seasonal contracts from November 1st to April 15th, giving you peace of mind all winter long. We also offer pay-per-visit options depending on availability.
